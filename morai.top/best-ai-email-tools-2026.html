Best AI Email Tools 2026

AI-powered email tools that write better replies, prioritize intelligently, and automate the inbox tasks that eat your day.

How AI Email Tools Work

AI email tools use large language models to understand the content and context of your emails, then assist with composing replies, summarizing threads, scheduling meetings, and flagging urgent messages. The best tools integrate directly with Gmail and Outlook, requiring no workflow changes — the AI appears as a sidebar or compose assistant.

Top AI Email Tools

Gmail / Workspace

Gemini in Gmail

Google's Gemini AI built into Gmail. Write email drafts, summarize thread history, draft replies, and prioritize inbox — all without leaving Gmail. Available in Gemini for Workspace plans. Free trial available.

Outlook

Microsoft Copilot in Outlook

AI-powered email summaries, draft replies, and meeting scheduling in Outlook. Similar capability to Gemini for Gmail. Best for users already in the Microsoft ecosystem.

Standalone

Superhuman AI

Superhuman's AI features include "Remind Me Later," email triage, and AI-powered composition. Known for its keyboard-first interface and speed. Premium pricing.

Standalone

Shortwave

AI-first email client with GPT-4o-powered writing, smart summaries, and automatic scheduling. A modern email experience for teams wanting AI built into the client itself.

How We Test AI Email Tools

We test AI email tools across: writing quality (are AI replies natural and accurate?), time savings (does it actually reduce inbox time?), and integration (does it work without disrupting your workflow?). Tools that fail any of these criteria don't make our rankings.

Best Practices for AI Email

  • Review before sending — AI drafts are starting points, not final outputs. Always review before hitting send.
  • Use for routine responses — Meeting confirmations, status updates, and standard replies are where AI saves the most time.
  • Set smart rules — Configure AI to auto-sort newsletters and promotional emails, so you only see what matters.
  • Train the AI — Most tools learn from your writing style over time. The longer you use them, the better the output.
